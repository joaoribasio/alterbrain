# 0023. Learner-neutral core, programme notes and country packs

Status: accepted (2026-10-08, product owner)

Amendment (ADR 0027): the six lens briefs moved a second time, before release, to `.claude/skills/critique/references/lenses/` (ADR 0026), and the reviewers run on the named helpers `helper-review` and `helper-judgement` instead of the `lens` agent. Programme-note migration 0003 is now a guided migration, not a script. The decisions below are otherwise unchanged.

## Context
Alterbrain started as an MBA pilot. The core, the skills, the onboarding and the wording assumed one reader: an MBA student in the Netherlands who takes courses inside one programme at one school and looks for a job there. That reader is the first user, not the only one. People who learn and work differ in ways that change what the framework should ask and do:

- A university degree student has the same course machinery as an MBA student and no need for the business frameworks.
- An online learner (Coursera, edX and the like) has standalone courses with a provider instead of a school, often no stated rule on AI use, and deadlines that are self-paced.
- A working professional has no courses at all, but has focus areas and projects, and still wants notes, drafting, study and a job search.

Two files carried the MBA assumption in structure, not only in wording. The course procedure and the assignment studio lived in the MBA pack, although nothing in them is about business. The school and the programme sat in `config/brain.json` (`school.name`, `school.programme`), so a course could not say which programme it belonged to, and school-wide facts (the AI rule, the grading scale, the submission conventions) were copied into every course note. And the whole Netherlands job search was part of the MBA pack (ADR 0012), so the jobs skill could not serve anyone looking for work elsewhere.

The product owner decided on 2026-10-08 to make the core useful to anyone who learns, keep MBA as the first pack, build no further learner packs now, and make migrations a real part of every release (ADR 0024).

## Decision
- **The learner question, once, early.** Onboarding M2 asks what the user is learning or doing: an MBA, a university degree, online courses, working with no courses, or something else. The answer is stored as `learner: { kind, detail }` in `config/brain.json`, with kinds `mba`, `degree`, `online`, `professional` and `other`. `other` behaves like `degree` and keeps the user's own words in `detail`. The kind sets defaults: `online` has no programme note unless the courses form one track, runs the AI-rule check only where a rule exists, and asks for class days only for courses with live sessions; `professional` has no courses, programme or AI-rule steps. Skills and code read the kind through one rule, so an install without it still behaves (see Consequences).
- **Packs are read by code.** `config/brain.json` has a `packs` array of ids (template: `["core"]`). `learner.kind` drives the onboarding branches and the defaults, `packs` drives the content that is added, and the two are separate: a degree student can switch the MBA frameworks on at any time with `/reconfigure`. `onboard-seed.mjs` copies the frameworks of every listed pack and no longer carries a fixed MBA job.
- **The course machinery and the assignment studio are core.** The course procedure moves to `.claude/skills/course/references/course-setup.md` (the `/course` skill owns it). Ingest of course material, the after-class nudge (`system/lib/courses.mjs`), `/study` and the generic parts of `/assignment` (the lens briefs and the note templates) work for every learner. An assignment may have no course. Its target grade is asked in the course's own scale and stored on the 10-point scale. `system/packs/mba/` keeps what is specific to business: the frameworks library, the case method (`templates/case.md`) and business critique presets (`critique-presets.md`), together with MBA wording in the places that name it.
- **Programme notes, with courses kept flat.** A new optional note per programme, `vault/20_areas/programmes/<Programme name>.md` (for example `MBA – RSM`), holds the facts that are true for every course in it once: the school or provider, the level, start and end, the terms, the school-wide AI rule, the grading scale, the submission conventions and career services. A course in the programme links to it with `programme: "[[MBA – RSM]]"`. A standalone course (Coursera) has no programme and carries a `provider` property instead. Course folders stay flat in `vault/20_areas/courses/<course-slug>/`, with no programme name in the slug; only a clash adds a suffix (`Strategy (Coursera)`). The `school` block in `config/brain.json` is replaced by the programme note, and the old keys stay readable as a fallback.
- **Inheritance, with the course winning.** At course setup, Alterbrain copies the facts that the code reads from the course note alone: `ai_policy`, its quote (marked `Programme rule: `) and the term dates from the programme's Terms row. It reads the rest at the moment of use and never copies it: the grading scale, the submission conventions and career services, taking the course note's own section first. A later change to a programme note rewrites no course; the review mode lists the affected courses and asks once. The course's own `ai_policy` still wins and is still what rule 6 of `system/core.md` reads.
- **`none-stated`.** `ai_policy` gains a sixth value. It means that no rule was found in what was read (the course files, the programme note, and the provider's terms only if they were given), or that the user confirmed there is no rule. It does not mean that a provider was checked. Rule 6 says nothing for `allowed` and `none-stated`, and nothing for an assignment with no course. `unknown` keeps its warning.
- **Country packs.** Job hunting becomes a country pack under `system/packs/country-<cc>/`, with `<cc>` the lower-case ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code, starting with `country-nl`. A country pack has a `README.md` and a `jobs.md` whose headings are fixed: Defaults, Onboarding questions, Checks, Keep rules, Fields, Say once. The jobs skill is country-neutral. It finds the pack in this order: `packs` lists `country-<cc>` for `jobs.country`, then the user's own `.claude/skills/my-country-<cc>/jobs.md`, then an old-install fallback, then none (the core flow, with one line that checks for that country do not exist yet). The jobs scripts stay where they are, so permission lists and the user's own copies keep working.
- **Professionals.** A working professional keeps focus areas in `USER.md` and projects in notes of type `project` under `vault/10_projects/`. There is no learner pack for them: core defaults cover the case.
- **No new learner packs now.** Packs for undergraduates, online learners or professionals are not built ahead of demand. Real users will ask; self-build drafts a pack on request with `/propose`.

## Consequences
- Moved paths: the six lens briefs to `.claude/skills/assignment/references/lenses/` (then on to `.claude/skills/critique/references/lenses/`, see the amendment above), four note templates (`assignment`, `rubric`, `decisions`, `critique`) to `system/templates/notes/`, the course procedure to `.claude/skills/course/references/course-setup.md`, and the Netherlands job guides from `system/packs/mba/jobs-nl/` to `system/packs/country-nl/`. The CHANGELOG lists each under `### Moved`.
- Existing users do not change shape by accident. Migration 0002 (`learner-and-packs`) records `mba` and switches on the packs they already use; 0003 (`programme-note`) makes a programme note from the old `school` block and links their courses; 0004 reports `my-*` skills, helpers and identity notes that point to moved files; 0005 reports edits to framework files that now have a new place. Both of the first two only add: the old keys stay.
- Fallbacks, read until a later release removes them by migration (ADR 0024): no `learner` means `mba` when `packs` lists `mba` or a `school` block exists; `packs` missing means `core` only; `school.name` and `school.programme` serve as display text only when there is no programme note, and are never copied into a new course note; the `school` property of an older course note is a display fallback next to `programme` and `provider`; the pack for `jobs.country` is found without a `country-*` entry in `packs`. The SPEC lists each with the file that reads it and its test.
- The always-loaded context grew. `system/core.md` is 7,900 bytes, under the 7,918 it had before this change (the folder map and rule 6 gained lines and other wording was trimmed), but the empty identity templates together with it measure about 11.4 KB against the 8 KB the SPEC states. The cap is not enforced by a test. The SPEC flags the mismatch for the owner and proposes a figure (section 5).
- This ADR supersedes ADR 0012 in two places. Other countries no longer need a blueprint: a country pack is the route. And the remark that thresholds are stored in the wiki was never how it was built: the salary table is a framework file, `salary-thresholds.md`, now in `system/packs/country-nl/`. The rest of ADR 0012 (sources, checks, never auto-apply) stands. ADR 0022 names the course procedure at its old path; it stays as history, and the new path is the one above.
- Not tested with real users: an online learner, a working professional and a degree student through the full onboarding. The paths and defaults are checked by unit tests and by reading the procedures. The default of `mba` when nothing in the CV points elsewhere is a pilot default to revisit before a wider release.

## Alternatives considered
- **Nest course folders under programmes.** Gives the structure on disk, and breaks every link, the digest scan and the ingest course label; moving a course between programmes would move a folder. A link in the course note carries the same meaning and moves nothing. Rejected.
- **A `system/packs/learning` pack for the course machinery.** It would be on for everyone and so would not be a pack. Rejected: the machinery is core.
- **Packs now for degree, online and professional learners.** There is no evidence yet of what they need beyond the core defaults, and each pack is content to maintain. Rejected until a real user asks.
- **Rename the recipient classes or the application fields to neutral words.** They are stored in drafts and applications, so a rename would need migrations for no gain to the user. Rejected: the ids stay, and the definitions in `system/packs/twin/drafting.md` section 3 are widened instead.
- **Move the jobs scripts into the country pack.** It would churn the permission allowlist and break the user's own copies that call the scripts by path. Rejected: the scripts stay, and the pack holds the data they read.
