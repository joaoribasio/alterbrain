# People

Notes about people you work or study with: business information only (their role, company, how you met). Every fact carries a source and a date. Mark anyone who asked not to be contacted with `dnc: true`.

Each note can hold a small contact profile: when you last spoke, when to follow up, how often to keep in touch, and a log of what you talked about. Say "add Alex Doe to my contacts" or `/people` and I fill it in. The weekly review lists who is due. Birthdays and other personal details are stored only if you add them.

The table of everyone is in `vault/_views/Contacts.base`.
