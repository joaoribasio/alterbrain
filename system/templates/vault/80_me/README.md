# About me

This folder holds who you are, so Alterbrain can help in your voice and not make things up.

- `SOUL.md`: how Alterbrain should behave with you.
- `IDENTITY.md`: who Alterbrain is.
- `USER.md`: a short profile of you.
- `MEMORY.md`: things worth remembering.
- `fact-sheet.md`: facts about you that drafts may use.
- `voice/`: samples and notes about how you write.
- `brand/`: your colours and fonts for reports. It is your default look when no template is set.
- `templates/`: templates from your school, employer or provider (one folder each). Attach them to a course, programme or project with `/template`.

The onboarding chat (`/onboard`) fills these in. You can edit them any time.
