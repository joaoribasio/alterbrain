# VRIO

**Definition:** A resource gives lasting advantage only if it is Valuable, Rare, hard to Imitate, and the firm is Organised to use it.

## When to use / when not to
- Use it to find out what a firm really does better than rivals.
- Use it to challenge claims like "our people are our strength".
- Do not use it on the whole firm at once. Test one resource at a time.
- Do not use it without a rival to compare against.

## Inputs you need
- A list of the firm's resources (assets, skills, brand, data, relationships).
- Knowledge of what rivals have.
- How the firm is run: processes, incentives, structure.

## Step-by-step
1. List 5 to 8 key resources and capabilities.
2. For each, ask four questions in order:
   - **Valuable?** Does it help us use an opportunity or defend against a threat?
   - **Rare?** Do few or no rivals have it?
   - **Costly to Imitate?** Is it hard or slow to copy (history, links, complexity)?
   - **Organised?** Do we have the processes and culture to use it fully?
3. Stop at the first "no". That sets the result.
4. Read the result:
   - Not valuable: competitive **disadvantage**.
   - Valuable only: **parity**.
   - Valuable and rare: **temporary** advantage.
   - Valuable, rare, costly to imitate: **sustained** advantage, if organised to use it.
5. Say what the firm should protect and build.

## Common pitfalls (what professors mark down)
- Calling a generic thing a strength ("good staff") with no evidence it is rare.
- Skipping the imitation test and saying "we have a brand".
- Treating "valuable" as "we like it", not "customers pay for it".
- Looking at resources in isolation, not at how they combine.
- No conclusion about what to do.

## Worked example (synthetic)
A small Dutch bakery chain:

| Resource | V | R | I | O | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sourdough recipe | Yes | Yes | No (copied in 6 months) | Yes | Temporary advantage |
| Prime town-centre leases | Yes | Yes | Yes (long leases, few sites) | Yes | Sustained advantage |
| Friendly staff | Yes | No | No | Yes | Parity |
| Old ovens | No | No | No | No | Disadvantage |

Strategy: protect and extend the leases. Treat the recipe as a short head start.

## Questions to ask yourself
- Who else has this, and for how long?
- Why can't a rich rival simply buy or build it?
- Do we use it fully, or is it wasted?
- What would we lose if it left?
